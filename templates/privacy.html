Our Privacy Policy

Introduction

We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used and protected.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Details: Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address provided during order placement.
  • Payment Information: UPI IDs or payment references used for transactions (not stored by us directly).
  • Usage Data: Device type, browser, and interactions on our platform to improve services.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Process and deliver your food orders.
  • Communicate important updates regarding your order or account.
  • Maintain security and prevent fraud.
  • Enhance user experience and platform functionality.

Data Protection

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Payment transactions are encrypted, and sensitive financial data is not stored on our servers.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or trade personal data. Limited sharing may occur with trusted partners such as payment gateways or delivery staff, solely to complete your order.

Your Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting our support team.

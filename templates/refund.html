Refund Policy

At Den-qr, customer satisfaction is our top priority. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued for orders and subscription services.

1. Food Orders

  • Once an order has been placed and confirmed, it cannot be cancelled.
  • Refunds will only be considered in cases of:
    • Wrong items delivered.
    • Incomplete or missing items.
    • Non-delivery due to technical errors in the system.
  • All refund requests must be raised within 24 hours of the order time.

2. Subscription Plans

  • Subscription fees (Basic, Medium, Unlimited) are non-refundable once paid.
  • If technical issues prevent you from using your subscribed benefits, our support team will assist in resolving the issue.
  • Prorated refunds are not provided if you discontinue the service mid-term.

3. Payment Method

Refunds, if approved, will be processed through the same payment method (UPI, PhonePe, Google Pay, etc.) used at the time of purchase. Processing time may vary depending on the bank/payment provider.
